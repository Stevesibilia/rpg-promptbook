How files find their prompt

Midjourney

The filename holds the first words of the prompt. They pick the prompt, in any category: Scenes, PCs, NPCs or Handouts.

steve_collapsed_1900s_Sicilian_library_at_night_3f9a1c22-…_2.png
→ Scenes · S1 · prompt 1
steve_portrait_of_an_old_Sicilian_priest_c41d07e2-…_0.png
→ NPCs · Padre Anselmo
Suno

The filename is the track title from the prompt. A second take gets "(1)".

dominus deus sabaoth (1).mp3
→ S5 · Capo Peloro, la terza scossa
Two matches

When two prompts start with the same words, their files match both. They wait in Unmatched, marked "matches 2 prompts": pick the prompt there. Start each prompt differently to avoid it.

Other files

Renamed files, or prompts you changed in Midjourney or Suno, go to Unmatched. Assign them by hand.

No scenes for the board yet

The board makes one row per scene from the appendix Appendice: immagini e musica at the end of the document. This document has no appendix, or the appendix has no scenes.

Mockup notes
Screen
  • Route /projects/:id/board. Rail entry Images and music, between Research and Brainstorm log.
  • Categories: the switch at the top. Each tab shows its number of image prompts and what is still wanted; a blue dot means new files. The filters work inside the active tab.
  • Scenes: one row per ### S… of the appendix. The image half is a stack of prompts (S1 three, S4 two, S6 none), each with Copy, Edit, Remove, its files and its own status: Wanted (no files), Received (files, none chosen), Chosen. The music half is unchanged.
  • PCs, NPCs, Handouts: project-wide cards, a name and a prompt each. Thumbnails follow the prompt's --ar.
  • Click a thumbnail to open it; the round badge chooses it, one per prompt. Removing a prompt sends its images to Unmatched. Play only toggles (no audio).
  • No prompts yet: the prompts come from Claude through the connector; the request is copyable. Each tab has its empty state with an Add button.
  • No appendix: only Scenes needs it; PCs, NPCs and Handouts work without.
  • Dropping: real files dropped on this page also open the overlay, then the result.
  • After a drop: the result line links to the tabs, new files marked, Unmatched pinned at the top. One file matches two prompts; its menu lists those two first, then every prompt by category.
  • Matching help: the popover behind "How matching works", also linked from the Unmatched tray.
  • Existing boards: the scene's single prompt becomes its prompt 1. Nothing to show.
  • Desktop, offline: files dropped offline stay on this computer and upload later; choosing and prompts still work.
  • Phone width: the same board in a 390 px frame. A narrow browser window gets the same layout.
  • The half-circle in the top bar switches light and dark.
