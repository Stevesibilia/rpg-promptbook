  • Route /projects/:id/board. Rail entry Images and music, between Research and Brainstorm log.
  • Board: one row per ### S… of the appendix. Each half has the scene's visual: or mood: line (read-only), the prompt with Copy and Edit, who wrote it, the files, and a status: Wanted (no files), Received (files, none chosen), Chosen.
  • Click a thumbnail to open it; the round badge chooses it. Play only toggles (no audio). Filters keep rows with at least one half in that state; the other half dims.
  • No prompts yet: the prompts come from Claude through the connector; the request is copyable. Write by hand stays on every row.
  • Dropping: real files dropped on this page also open the overlay, then the result.
  • After a drop: the result line, new files marked, Unmatched pinned at the top while you scroll. Assigning moves a file into its row.
  • Matching help: the popover behind "How matching works", also linked from the Unmatched tray.
  • Desktop, offline: files dropped offline stay on this computer and upload later; choosing and prompts still work.
  • Phone width: the same board in a 390 px frame. A narrow browser window gets the same layout.
  • The half-circle in the top bar switches light and dark.
